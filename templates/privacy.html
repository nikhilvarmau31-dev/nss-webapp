Privacy Policy

Data Protection & Privacy Notice for NSS Volunteers & Portal Users

Home Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 8 November 2026 • MVGRCE(A) NSS Cell

1. Information We Collect

To administer voluntary community service hours, government NSS certification, and emergency blood bank registries, the MVGR NSS portal collects:

  • Identity & Academic Data: Student Name, College Roll Number, Academic Year, and Department Branch.
  • Contact Information: Institutional Email Address, Contact Phone Number, and Residential/Hostel Address.
  • Health & Humanitarian Data: Blood Group (used exclusively for emergency voluntary donor registries and verified medical camp rosters).

2. Purpose of Data Processing

All volunteer information collected through this portal is processed strictly for official educational, humanitarian, and institutional purposes, including:

  • Accreditation of volunteer community service hours toward graduation requirements.
  • Coordination of community events, tree plantation drives, and rural residential camps.
  • Facilitating rapid voluntary blood donor dispatch during emergency district health requirements.
  • Government audit reporting to Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University (JNTU) and Regional Directorate of NSS.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement strict administrative and database encryption controls to safeguard your personal records against unauthorized access, modification, or exposure. Your personal data is never sold, leased, or distributed to third-party commercial entities.

4. Faculty & Volunteer Access Rights

Enrolled volunteers have full access to view their recorded hours, update contact information, and review their participation status via the Student Dashboard. Faculty coordinators review service records under strict institutional confidentiality guidelines.

5. Contact the Privacy Desk

For data inquiries or record modifications, please contact the MVGR NSS Unit:

NSS Cell Office: Admin Block, MVGR College of Engineering (A), Chintalavalasa, Vizianagaram.
Email: nsscell@mvgrce.edu.in / support.nssmvgr@gmail.com
