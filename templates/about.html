Our Story

What is NSS?

The National Service Scheme (NSS) was launched in 1969 with the primary objective of developing the personality and character of the student youth through voluntary community service. The motto of NSS is "Not Me But You", which reflects the essence of democratic living and inculcates the spirit of selfless service among the youth.

NSS aims to establish the link between the student youth in the campus and the community at large. NSS volunteers work hard to ensure that everyone who is needy gets help without having to ask for it. NSS is not about serving the society for name or fame.

"Not Me But You"

Objectives

  • Understand the community in which students work
  • Identify needs and problems of the community
  • Develop among themselves a sense of social & civic responsibility
  • Utilize knowledge in finding practical solutions
  • Develop competence required for group living
  • Gain skills in mobilizing community participation

NSS Symbol & Badge

The Wheel

The NSS badge has the Konark Wheel (Rath Chakra) from the Sun Temple of Konark. The wheel represents the cycle of life — creation and protection. It symbolizes continuity, motion, and progress.

The Red Color

The red color in the NSS badge denotes the energy and spirit of youth. It symbolizes passion and dedication to serve.

The Blue Color

The navy blue color represents the cosmos of which the earth is a small part — NSS volunteer considers his/her good work as a humble contribution to society.

Our Activities

Regular Activities (Throughout the year)

Community development work, awareness programs, blood donation, cleanliness drives, survey activities, and adult education. Each student must complete 120 hours per year.

Special Camp (7 Days)

An annual residential special camp is organized in an adopted village. Volunteers stay in the village and work on specific projects. This earns additional credit hours.

Republic Day Parade

Selected NSS volunteers get the opportunity to participate in the Republic Day Parade at Kartavya Path (formerly Rajpath), New Delhi — one of the highest honors for an NSS volunteer.
